Old Soul

Think 1960s, listening to Nina Simone singing about feelings accompanied with some of the best jazz pianists, then swipe to the next song and then you are with Otis Redding singing about change. Then you slow it down even further to the era of soft jazz in the 1940s and you find yourself just relaxing in your room, either settling down after a long day, finished a shower and a cold milo can. The oldies take you to a place where you can kick back and just unwind.


Mainstream

Despite what others say, mainstream is mainstream for a reason. It's just plain simple good music that everyone today has heard before, even if it was on socials or even on the speaker somewhere. You will be guaranteed to meet many who knows these songs. Think Tracy Chapman, "Fast Car". Or even Adele "Someone Like You", or more modern we have any Billie Eillish song. More recent we even have Olivia Dean. These are the type of music you can easily play on drives and gaurantee everyone is going to sing along with.


When listening to music it really doesn't matter what the songs are, it's more what type of mood do I want to set. Right now I want to go to sleep so slow 1960s songs will deffs be great. Mood + Right Song = Better and enhances experience.
