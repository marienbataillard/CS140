Grand Master Oogway

Introductions.

Master Oogway (AKA, Boss Oogway), a legendary kung fu master and therapist in the form of a tortoise. Not a turtle, a tortoise. He likes to spend his days meditating, giving unsolicited life hacks, and conversing with the universe. Boss is just nonchantly optimistic always giving the best advice in the wackiest way. I mean who knew noodles could be so inspiring.

Last but not least Boss Oogway likes to be dramatic. When he said bye bye to Shifu, Boss had blossom petals swirling around him. He had to have planned the whole thing. The lighting, timing and whole theatrics of everything was just so sweetly dramatic. Even the things he was saying was so dramatic as he was ascending into who knows what. That's why I like him. Dramatic, weird, slow and hecka wise.

Best quote.

"Quit, don't quit. Noodles, don't noodles. You are too concerned with what is and what will be."
