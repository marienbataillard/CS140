Grand Master Oogway

Introductions.

Master Oogway (AKA, Boss Oogway), a legendary kung fu master and therapist in the form of a tortoise. Not a turtle, a Tortoise. He loves using silly sentences that change your perspective on life. He likes to keep everyone on their toes, surprising them with the random decisions that don't make sense but somehow works out. E.g A panda for the dragon warrior? Who would've guessed.

Last but not least Boss Oogway likes pretty. When he first said bye bye to Shifu, which was lowkey his death. Boss had blossom petals swirling around him after his iconic speech. He had to have planned the whole thing. The life of being a kung fu master = not pretty. So might as well end it pretty. So I believe Boss liked pretty looking things like how he liked to use pretty sounding words.
