Muri Beach

In the Cook Islands, the capital island of Rarotonga is where I live. Down the road from my house is the very famous Muri Beach. Muri Beach is well known because of what we call motu's, it is the only area on the island where we have islets.

I grew up swimming there, it's beautiful and is a great tour attraction and since it is on the east side the sun rise is very beautiful. Theres also a steep hiking hill that I love to go up to catch the sunrise with my dog Kina. We often have to set out around 4am since the sun comes up early. The hike is dangerous but the view is stunning.
